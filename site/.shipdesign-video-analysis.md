## Motion observations

- The recording represents scroll-scrubbed or carefully staged scroll-triggered scenes, not an autoplay-only animation.
- Sticky-feeling focal zones hold the primary statement while secondary material enters around it.
- Reveals use opacity plus modest vertical translation and clipping; there is no aggressive scaling or bounce.
- Text choreography is sequential: headline first, supporting explanation second, proof object third.
- Scene transitions preserve a shared vertical axis so one section hands off to the next.
- Pauses/holds are meaningful: the primary statement remains readable before the next layer arrives.
- Motion is predominantly ease-out for entrances and near-linear for scrubbed progress.

## Transfer

- Pin the orchestration board while its active stage progresses.
- Highlight one workflow stage at a time, then leave a confirmed state behind.
- Reveal related specialist chips only when their stage becomes active.
- Use a modest clip/translate reveal for ordinary sections so the page gains continuity without making every block cinematic.
- Preserve a shared center line between workflow states and the next section.
- Let scroll velocity control progression naturally; do not hijack scrolling.

## Do not copy

- Source brand, copy, logos, illustrations, exact highlight treatment, exact timings, exact scene order, or any recognizable signature sequence.
- Autoplay video as the product experience.
- Decorative floating marks or parallax applied everywhere.

## Adapted motion direction: Routing Handoff

ShipDesign uses scroll as a handoff signal. The workflow board stays visually stable while FRAME, EVIDENCE, DIRECTION, BUILD, MOTION, REVIEW, QUALITY GATE, and SHIP become active in order. Each completed stage receives a quiet confirmation; its related upstream source chips gain the acid signal. The next section enters with a restrained reveal, so the page feels like one orchestration run rather than disconnected panels.

## Motion posture

- Intensity: 4/10.
- Scroll dependency: workflow progression is scroll-linked through IntersectionObserver; section reveals are once-only viewport triggers.
- Timing: 180-700ms for UI/reveal transitions; no artificial delays longer than the stagger needed to establish order.
- Easing: existing strong ease-out token for entrances; no bounce.
- Distance / scale: 18-28px vertical travel, no scale except a subtle active marker emphasis.
- Sticky positioning: workflow content only, desktop/tablet; mobile uses normal flow with active-state highlighting.
- Parallax: rejected; the product is a routing system, not an image scene.
- Stagger: only workflow rows/chips and section children, 60-90ms visual rhythm.
- Masking: CSS clip-path/overflow reveal for section entrances only.
- Reduced motion: all content remains visible, workflow is static, color/state cues remain.
